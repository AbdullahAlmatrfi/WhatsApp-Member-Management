# GymConnect - Reception Staff Guide

This guide is for the person at the front desk. No technical knowledge needed.

GymConnect keeps your members' names and phone numbers in one place, and helps you send the same WhatsApp message to many of them.

Arabic version: [STAFF-GUIDE.ar.md](STAFF-GUIDE.ar.md)

---

## The one thing to remember

**Members are removed automatically after a few days.** By default, each member disappears **3 days (72 hours) after you added them**. This is normal. It is not a bug, and you did not do anything wrong.

The note under the "Members" title always shows the current time limit. If you want to keep a copy of your list, press **Download all** (see section 7 below).

---

## 1. Logging in

Your manager (the admin) gives you an email and a password. There is no sign-up button, so you cannot create your own account.

1. Open the GymConnect link your manager gave you.
2. Press **Staff Login**.
3. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
4. Press **Sign in**.

To leave, press the **Sign out** icon (arrow leaving a door) at the top. Do this on shared computers.

## 2. Adding a member

1. In the **Add New Member** box, type the member's **name**. A short label like "1" also works.
2. Type their **mobile number**: 9 digits starting with 5 (for example 5XXXXXXXX). The +966 is already there, so do not type it.
3. Press **Add Member**.

You will see "Member added successfully". If the number is already saved, you will see "Number already exists".

## 3. Finding a member

Type a name or part of a number in the **Search** box above the list. The list shrinks as you type. Clear the box to see everyone again.

Each member shows a small tag such as "Added today" or "Added 2 days ago". If you see **leaving soon**, that member will be removed automatically very soon.

## 4. Sending a message to many members (Broadcast)

1. Press the green **Broadcast** button at the top.
2. In **Compose message**, write your message. Type `{name}` where you want each member's first name. Example: `Dear {name}, your membership ends soon.` Each person gets their own name.
3. Under **Recipients**, choose who to message:
   - **Not messaged** = people you have not messaged yet.
   - **Messaged** = people you already messaged.
4. Tap the people you want to message. Or press **Select all shown** to pick everyone in the list.
5. Press **Start broadcast (number)**. The button stays grey until you have written a message and chosen at least one person.
6. You will see the first person and their message. Press **Open and send**. WhatsApp opens with the message already typed for that person.
7. **In WhatsApp, press Send.** Then come back to GymConnect.
8. Press **Open and send** for the next person. Repeat until you see "Broadcast finished". Press **Done**.

Need to fix the message or change who is selected? Press **Back to edit**. Your message and the people you have not messaged yet stay as they were. People already opened are removed from the selection, so you will not message them twice by accident.

### Please read: GymConnect does not press Send for you

GymConnect only opens the WhatsApp chat with the message ready. **You must press Send in WhatsApp every time.** If you forget, the message is not sent.

### What "Messaged" really means

A member is marked **Messaged** when GymConnect **opened their WhatsApp chat**. It does **not** prove the message was sent or delivered. If you opened a chat and then did not press Send, they are still marked Messaged.

To start a new round (a new offer, for example), open **Broadcast** and press **Reset (new promotion)**. This clears all the "Messaged" marks. It cannot be undone.

Photos and videos are not supported yet. Only text is sent.

## 5. Deleting members

**One member:** press the red bin icon on their card, then **Delete**.

**Many members:**
1. Press **Select**.
2. Tap each member you want to remove. Or press **Select all shown**.
3. Press the red **Delete (number)** button.
4. Check the number in the question, then press **Delete**.

Deleting cannot be undone. To leave Select mode without deleting, press **Cancel**.

## 6. WhatsApp from one member's card

The green WhatsApp icon on a member's card opens an empty chat with them (no ready message).

## 7. Keeping a copy: Download all

Press **Download all** above the list. A file (CSV) with name, phone, status and date added is saved to your computer. You can open it with Excel or Google Sheets. Do this before the members' time runs out if you need the list later.

## 8. Settings: language, look and WhatsApp type

Press the **Settings** icon (cogwheel) at the top.

- **Language**: English or العربية. Arabic switches the page to right-to-left.
- **Appearance**: **Dark** or **Light**.
- **WhatsApp**: **WhatsApp Web** (opens in your browser) or **WhatsApp Desktop** (opens the WhatsApp app on your computer). Choose the one you normally use.

These choices are remembered on that device.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I cannot log in ("Wrong email or password") | Check for typing mistakes. If it still fails, ask your manager (admin) to reset your password. |
| It says it cannot connect | Check your internet and try again. |
| A member has disappeared | This is the automatic clean-up, not a mistake. Members are removed after the time limit shown above the list. Check your Download all file. Add the member again if needed. |
| The member list will not load | Press **Try again**. Nothing was lost. |
| I pressed "Open and send" but the message was not sent | GymConnect only opens the chat. Go to WhatsApp and press **Send**. |
| Nothing opens when I press "Open and send" | Your browser blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for this site, then press **Open and send** again. Also check WhatsApp Web is signed in. |
| Someone is marked Messaged but never got the message | Marked means "chat opened" only. Send them a message by hand using their card's WhatsApp icon. |
| The page is in the wrong language | Settings, then **Language**. |
| Something else looks wrong | Ask your manager (admin). |
