# GymConnect - Owner (Admin) Guide

This guide is for the gym owner or manager. You control who can log in, what the app is called, and how long members are kept. No technical knowledge needed.

Arabic version: [ADMIN-GUIDE.ar.md](ADMIN-GUIDE.ar.md)

When you sign in, you land on the **Admin** page (the control panel). The staff screen is a separate view, and you can switch to it at any time (see "Your own members" below).

At the top of the Admin page you will see an **Overview**: how many members there are, how many are marked Messaged, how many staff accounts you have, and how many of the members are private (only you).

---

## 1. Create the staff login

Your gym has **one staff account** for the person at reception. There is no public sign-up, so nobody can join unless you create their login.

1. On the Admin page, find the **Add staff** box.
2. Type their **Name (optional)**, for example "Ahmed". It is used for the greeting at the top of their screen.
3. Type their **Email**.
4. Type a **Password** (at least 8 characters), or press **Generate** to get a strong one made for you.
5. Press **Create account**.

A card called **Staff account ready** appears with the email and password.

## 2. Send the login to your staff member

On that card:

- Press **Copy** to copy the email and password, then paste them into a message.
- Or press **Send on WhatsApp** to open WhatsApp with the details already typed.

**Important:** this is the only time you see the password on screen. If you close the card without sharing it, just reset the password (section 3) to make a new one.

Tell your staff member to open the GymConnect link and press **Staff Login**.

## 3. Reset a forgotten password

1. In **Staff accounts**, find the staff person.
2. Press the **key** icon.
3. Confirm with **Reset password**.

A new password is made, and the old one stops working straight away. Share the new one with the **Copy** or **Send on WhatsApp** buttons, the same as before.

## 4. Replace the staff person (one staff per gym)

Only one staff account is allowed. When the person leaves, or you want a different person:

1. In **Staff accounts**, press the **bin** icon next to the current staff account.
2. Confirm with **Delete**. They lose access immediately. This cannot be undone.
3. The **Add staff** box comes back. Create the new login (section 1).

Until you delete the current staff account, the Add staff box shows the message: "One staff account per gym. Delete the current one to add a different person."

## 5. Names: gym name and display names

- **Gym name**: type it in the **Gym name** box and press **Save**. It shows at the top of the screen for your staff.
- **Display name** (the name in the greeting, such as "Good morning, Ahmed"): in **Staff accounts**, press the **pencil** icon next to a person, type the name, and press **Save**. This works for the staff account and also for your own row.

## 6. Choose how long members are kept (auto-delete)

Members are **deleted automatically** a set time after they were added. This protects your members' phone numbers. You choose the time in **Auto-delete window**:

**7 hours, 24 hours, 2 days, or 3 days** (3 days is the default).

The time is counted from the moment each member was added.

**Warning: choosing a shorter time deletes members sooner.** If you pick a shorter option, the app asks "Shorten the auto-delete window?". Anyone added longer ago than the new limit will be removed within the hour, and this cannot be undone. Press **Shorten anyway** only if you are sure, or **Cancel**. Choosing a longer time saves straight away.

Tell your staff about this. They can press **Download all** on their screen to keep a copy of the list.

## 7. Your own members are private

Members that **you** add are **private to you**. Your staff member cannot see them. You can see everything: your own members and the ones staff added.

To add members yourself, press **Staff view** at the top of the Admin page. Add, search, delete and broadcast work exactly as described in the Staff Guide. To come back, press the **shield** icon at the top. Signing out and back in also takes you to the Admin page.

The Overview shows how many members are private, for example "3 private (only you)".

## 8. Language, look, and signing out

Press the **cogwheel** for **Settings**: language (English or العربية), **Dark** or **Light**. Press the **Sign out** icon when you are done on a shared computer.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Staff cannot log in | Reset their password (section 3) and send them the new one. Check they type the email exactly as shown on the card. |
| Staff forgot the password | Same: reset it (section 3). The old password cannot be shown again. |
| I cannot add a second staff person | Only one staff account is allowed. Delete the current one first (section 4), then add the new person. |
| It says "That email already has an account" | Use a different email. |
| It says the password is too short | Use at least 8 characters, or press **Generate**. |
| Staff say members disappeared | That is auto-delete. Check the time limit in **Auto-delete window**. Staff can press **Download all** to keep a copy. |
| "Couldn't load the console" or "Couldn't save" | Check the internet and press **Try again**. Nothing was lost. |
| "Server key isn't set yet" | The app setup is not finished. Ask the person who set up the app to add the key in Netlify. |
| Saving the gym name is turned off | The current name could not be loaded. Press **Try again**. |
| I see an old account marked "Pending" | Press **Approve** to make it the staff account (only if there is no staff account yet), or press the bin icon to remove it. |
